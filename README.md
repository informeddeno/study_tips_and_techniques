# Health
> ## from **Marvin Kangwana**

**Health** is a state of physical, mental, and social well-being.
Maintaining good health is essential for living a productive and fulfilling life.

### Why Health Matters
- It improves energy and daily performance.
- It reduces the risk of diseases.
- It supports mental clarity and emotional stability.
- It increases overall life expectancy.

### Key Areas of Health
1. **Physical Health** – Exercise, nutrition, and sleep.
2. **Mental Health** – Stress management and emotional balance.
3. **Social Health** – Healthy relationships and communication.

### Simple Daily Habits
- Drink enough water.
- Exercise for at least 30 minutes.
- Eat balanced meals.
- Get 7–9 hours of sleep.
- Take short breaks to reduce stress.

### Quick Example Plan

```text
Morning: 10-minute stretch + healthy breakfast
Afternoon: Balanced lunch + short walk
Evening: Light exercise + early sleep
Prioritize the most important subjects and **tackle challenging topics when your focus is strongest**.
